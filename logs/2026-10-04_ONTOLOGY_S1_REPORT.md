## What this means

- **The ontology made the alert queue shorter and cleaner, with higher pooled recall in this pool.** Alerts fell from 5015 to 4000 (−20.2 %); true alerts went from 2065 to 2139 in total, though some attacks caught by provenance alone were missed (true alerts fell in two base models; see the table). Precision rose by +12.3 points and the gain generalises across tasks (part a).
- **Recall is an observation about this pool, not a guarantee.** Per base model the change ranged from −3.7 to +3.7 points. Secondary, not part of the verdict: task-crossed recall non-inferiority p = 0.077 at 3 points and 0.030 at 5 points (95 % CI of the recall change −5.5 to +12.2); assumption-conditioned sign-flip over 6 base models p = 0.031. A recall claim for new tasks would need more tasks, not more runs (plan § Not in scope).
- **Not arbitrary:** randomly retyping tools and parameters with the same sizes gives mean precision 0.375 (part c).
- 13 of the 18 cohorts are defence or attack variants of gpt-4o-2024-05-13, whose undefended runs were in the v2 test; for that model what was held out is the variants, and it counts as one base model in (b).
